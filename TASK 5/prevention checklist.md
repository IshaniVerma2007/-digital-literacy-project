# Stay Safe Online: Student Prevention Checklist

UPI PIN Safety: Never enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money; a PIN is only required for sending or authorizing a payment.

Verify QR Codes: Do not scan QR codes sent via WhatsApp or SMS from unknown individuals, as these are often disguised "collection" requests.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Enable 2FA on your university email, GitHub, and social media accounts to provide an extra layer of security beyond your password.

Phishing Awareness: Avoid clicking on "urgent" links in emails or messages that claim your bank account is blocked or that you have won a prize.

Secure Connections: Only enter personal or financial information on websites that use https:// and show a secure padlock icon in the browser bar.

Public Wi-Fi Risks: Avoid accessing your bank portal or making online purchases while connected to open public Wi-Fi networks.

Strong Password Policy: Use unique, complex passwords for different platforms and avoid using easily guessable information like your birthdate or "123456

Privacy Settings: Regularly review the privacy settings on your social media profiles to limit what information is visible to the public or strangers.
