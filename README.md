## 📌 Project Description

This project is developed as part of the Digital Literacy course. The objective is to understand and demonstrate essential digital skills required for academic and professional success in today’s digital world.

As a Student Digital Ambassador, this portfolio focuses on spreading awareness about:

* Safe internet usage
* Professional online presence
* Digital communication
* Cybersecurity awareness

The project consists of five tasks, each representing a module of digital literacy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* To understand the concept of digital literacy
* To build a professional online identity
* To explore digital platforms for learning and collaboration
* To practice formal digital communication
* To create awareness about cybercrime and online safety
